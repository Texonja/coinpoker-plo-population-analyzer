INTERPRETATION

What changes in a three-bet pot?

IP bet frequencies were close in single-raised and three-bet pots (40.7% and 39.0%). Once OOP faced a bet, check-raises occurred in 5.1% of single-raised pots and 23.0% of three-bet pots. The latter estimate comes from 113 responses. Stack depth, board and bet size can differ between the groups.

The historical pool contains aggregate rows from a private export. The hand examples come from a separate public demo and illustrate the counting rule. The demo is small and selected for study. The dashboard’s reference ranges are set by hand and its sample tiers use opportunity counts alone. Estimating bluff profitability requires ranges, sizing and later-street outcomes.
