# When the raiser checks

A PL50 study of heads-up flops in CoinPoker PLO4. The last preflop raiser checks out of position (OOP). How often does the player in position (IP) bet, and how does OOP respond?

**40.7%** of single-raised opportunities produced an IP bet in the historical pool. The source row contains **345 bets + 502 checks = 847 opportunities**.

## Follow one hand

**#9000002879 · 8♦ Q♣ K♦ · ₮4.98 on the flop.** The small blind raised preflop. The hijack bets half pot after the small blind checks. That adds one IP bet. The small blind folds, adding one OOP fold. ## Published counts and public replay

The historical column uses the published PL50 aggregate CSV. The independent replay scans all 116 eligible PL50 hands in the 320-hand Leak Finder demo. Each column has its own denominator.

| Pot | Decision | Historical pool | Public replay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-raised pot | IP after a check | bet 345/847 (40.7%) · check 502/847 (59.3%) | bet 4/5 · check 1/5 |
| Single-raised pot | OOP facing a bet | fold 181/370 (48.9%) · call 170/370 (45.9%) · raise 19/370 (5.1%) | fold 2/5 · call 2/5 · raise 1/5 |
| Three-bet pot | IP after a check | bet 138/354 (39.0%) · check 216/354 (61.0%) | bet 1/2 · check 1/2 |
| Three-bet pot | OOP facing a bet | fold 42/113 (37.2%) · call 45/113 (39.8%) · raise 26/113 (23.0%) | fold 1/1 · call 0/1 · raise 0/1 |

Hero’s actions are excluded separately for each decision. A Hero bet can still produce a counted opponent response. A check-back counts once for IP and creates no OOP response opportunity. The private hands behind the historical aggregate are unavailable for individual replay. [Inspect the historical CSV.](demo/pool_summary.csv)

IP bet frequencies were close in single-raised and three-bet pots (40.7% and 39.0%). Once OOP faced a bet, check-raises occurred in 5.1% of single-raised pots and 23.0% of three-bet pots. The latter estimate comes from 113 responses. Stack depth, board and bet size can differ between the groups.
